About us

Rooted in Jhargram, built on real experience.

AgriOS Integrated Farm Pvt Ltd is a commercial integrated farm on 16 bigha of land at Andharia, Binpur — bringing scientific, sustainable farming to the heart of West Bengal.

Our story

The farm brings together poultry, livestock, aquaculture and horticulture into a single, deliberately connected system. Rather than depending on any one product or season, AgriOS spreads risk across many enterprises — while letting each one strengthen the others.

The result is a farm that's more resilient, more sustainable, and better for the land it sits on. Our long-term vision is to grow into a model farm, a training centre, and a trusted brand for farm-fresh produce.

The promoter

Led by Soumen Patra, who brings hands-on farm experience across pig, poultry and fish farming, plus formal training in scientific goat rearing from ICAR–IVRI, Kolkata.

What we do

Nine enterprises, one connected system.

Each unit earns on its own — and feeds the others. Here's how the farm fits together.

🐔

Contract poultry

Our anchor enterprise: broiler farming under a full-integrator model that provides chicks, feed, medicine and technical support, with an assured buyer. Starts at 5,000 birds per batch.

🐖

Pig farming

A scientific breeding unit built to modern standards — starting with a breeding herd and growing toward commercial production over five years.

🐐

Goat farming

A semi-intensive breeding unit for meat and stock, backed by formal ICAR training, with proper housing, fodder and health protocols.

🦆

Duck farming

100 ducks integrated with the fish ponds — droppings feed the ponds, and the flock gives both eggs and meat.

🐟

Aquaculture

Four scientifically managed ponds running composite and polyculture fish farming — Rohu, Catla, Mrigal, Grass Carp, Tilapia and more.

🍌

Horticulture

Banana and Red Lady papaya as the main crops, guava as a long-term orchard, plus coconut and black pepper along the boundary.

🐝

Beekeeping

Starting with 5 hives and scaling up — for honey, beeswax and better pollination across the farm's crops.

🌿

Vermicompost

Turning poultry litter and animal manure into high-value organic compost — for our own crops and for sale.

🌾

AgriOS Feed

Manufacturing our own animal feed on a proprietary in-house formula — for our units and to sell to other farmers under the AgriOS brand.

Sustainability

Better for the land, better for business

Integration isn't just good farming — it's good economics. Recycling waste cuts input costs, reduces chemical use, and builds soil health year after year.

  • ✓ Closed-loop waste recycling across every unit
  • ✓ Reduced dependence on chemical fertilizer
  • ✓ Rainwater harvesting and water reuse
  • ✓ Renewable energy planned as the farm grows
  • ✓ Boundary plantation for green cover and income

Common questions

Frequently asked

Can I visit the farm?
Yes — we welcome visits from buyers, partners and integrators. Use the "Schedule a farm visit" button above or message us on WhatsApp, and we'll arrange a convenient time.
Do you supply outside Jhargram?
Our farm is in Jhargram, West Bengal. For breeding stock, produce and feed, we can discuss supply to nearby districts depending on quantity and logistics — get in touch and we'll work it out.
Is your feed available to other farmers?
Yes. We manufacture our own feed on a proprietary in-house formula, and it's available to other farmers under our own brand. Contact us for the range, availability and pricing.
When will your products be available to buy?
Breeding stock (piglets, goat kids, ducklings, fingerlings) will be available fairly soon. Farm produce and farm-fresh goods follow as each unit matures. Leave your details and we'll notify you.
Are you open to integrator or business partnerships?
Absolutely. We run contract broiler poultry and are open to discussing partnerships across our enterprises. Reach out and we'll set up a conversation.
